# Privacy Policy for Sudoku

*Last updated: October 5, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sudoku app ("the App") handles your information.

## Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. No accounts are required and no data is sent to any external servers.

## Local Storage

The App stores game preferences and progress locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to anyone other than you.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
